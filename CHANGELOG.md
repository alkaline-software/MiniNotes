# Changelog

## v0.5.0

1. **Onyx.** MiniNotes is Onyx: a note-taking app of its own, built anew in Rust and GPUI, with the window, the settings, the motion and the languages of the Alkaline Software apps.
2. **A vault.** Notes are plain markdown files in a folder of your own. The sidebar is that folder: its notes and subfolders, new ones made in place, renamed, or moved to the trash, and whatever another app does there shows at once.
3. **Live preview.** A note is drawn as it reads while you write it: headings, bold and italic, lists with their bullets, numbers and boxes, quotes, code on a plate, tables as a grid, rules and links, with the marks of whatever the caret is in shown as written, as Obsidian does.
4. **Three ways to see a note.** As it reads while editing, as it is written, or as it reads alone (⌘E), from the strip or the View menu.
5. **Keys.** ⌘B and ⌘I toggle bold and italic, ⌘↩ a task's box, Tab and ⇧Tab a list's level, ↩ carries a list or a quote on, and lists renumber themselves.
6. **Fonts and size.** A note set in Inter, Source Serif or the system's face, code in JetBrains Mono or the system's monospace, at three sizes, in a readable column or the window's width.
7. **Eight languages.** English, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Japanese, Korean, French, German and Spanish, following the system's.
8. **Updates.** Onyx checks GitHub for a newer release once a day and installs it at a press, with no Sparkle feed.
