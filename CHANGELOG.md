# Changelog

## v0.5.0

1. **Onyx.** MiniNotes is Onyx: a note-taking app of its own, built anew in Rust and GPUI, with the window, the settings, the motion and the languages of the Alkaline Software apps.
2. **A vault.** Notes are plain markdown files in a folder of your own, made new in iCloud Drive or wherever you say, or opened as it is. A note is written when its tab closes or at ⌘S, never while you type. The sidebar is that folder: its notes and subfolders, new ones made in place, renamed, or moved to the trash, and whatever another app does there shows at once. With a vault and no note, the page greets you by name over the icon's picture in lines, with what to do next under it as Obsidian's new tab has it.
3. **Live preview.** A note is drawn as it reads while you write it: headings, bold and italic, lists with their bullets, numbers and boxes, quotes, code on a plate with its language's colors and its language's mark, tables as a grid, rules and links, with the marks of whatever the caret is in shown as written, as Obsidian does.
4. **Tabs.** Every open note is a tab as wide as its name, and a folder's tabs stand together on one pill, as emaki's sessions do by project. A tab is dragged along its pill and a pill along the row. ⌘W closes the tab, then the window; ⌘⇧T brings the last one back; ⌘1 to ⌘9 go straight to one, and ⌘ held shows which. The tabs open again at the next launch in their order.
5. **Three ways to see a note.** As it reads while editing, as it is written, or as it reads alone: ⌥1, ⌥2 and ⌥3, ⌘E to Read and back, the strip or the View menu.
6. **Keys.** ⌘B and ⌘I toggle bold and italic, ⌘↩ a task's box, Tab and ⇧Tab a list's level, ↩ carries a list or a quote on, and lists renumber themselves.
7. **Fonts and size.** A note set in one of eight faces, four serifs and four sans serifs, code in one of four monospace faces, as emaki offers them; the size a preset or your own for the note and for code, Chinese in a carried face of your choice (Noto Serif SC, Noto Sans SC, LXGW WenKai) or one you name from the Mac, in a readable column or the window's width.
8. **Eight languages.** English, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Japanese, Korean, French, German and Spanish, following the system's.
9. **Updates.** Onyx checks GitHub for a newer release once a day and installs it at a press, with no Sparkle feed.
