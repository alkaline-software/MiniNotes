# Changelog

## v0.5.0

1. **Onyx.** MiniNotes is Onyx: a note-taking app of its own, built anew in Rust and GPUI, with the window, the settings, the motion and the languages of the Alkaline Software apps.
2. **A vault.** Notes are plain markdown files in a folder of your own, made new in iCloud Drive or wherever you say, or opened as it is. A note is written when its tab closes or at ⌘S, never while you type. The sidebar is that folder, laid out as emaki's: the New Note and New Folder rows, the notes card with its notes and subfolders, new ones made in place, renamed, or moved to the trash, and whatever another app does there shows at once, and at the foot your picture and name. Drag its edge to widen it, fold it or bring it back, and double-click the edge to fit your notes' names. Folded, it is a rail with the traffic lights stacked at its head. ⌘K searches every note's name and words. With a vault and no note, the page greets you by name over the icon's picture in lines, with what to do next under it as Obsidian's new tab has it.
3. **Live preview.** A note is drawn as it reads while you write it: headings, bold and italic, lists with their bullets, numbers and boxes, quotes, code on a plate with its language's colors and its language's mark, tables as a grid, rules and links, with the marks of whatever the caret is in shown as written, as Obsidian does.
4. **Tabs.** A home tab at the very left shows the vault's page with every tab left open. Every open note is a tab as wide as its name, and a folder's tabs stand together on one pill, as emaki's sessions do by project. A tab is dragged along its pill and a pill along the row. ⌘W closes the tab, then the window; ⌘⇧T brings the last one back; ⌘1 to ⌘9 go straight to one, and ⌘ held shows which. The tabs open again at the next launch in their order.
5. **Three ways to see a note.** As it reads while editing, as it is written, or as it reads alone, drawn the same way each time: ⌃1, ⌃2 and ⌃3, ⌘E to Read and back, the colored square on the tabs' row or the View menu. Notes run Narrow, Middle or Wide, ⌥1, ⌥2 and ⌥3. Both are one choice for the whole app, kept until you change it. ⌘F finds a phrase in the note. The name over a note's words is its file's name: type there to rename it. A right click has a menu everywhere it means something: the words, a link, a text field, a note, a folder, a tab, the path.
6. **Keys.** ⌘B and ⌘I toggle bold and italic, ⌘↩ a task's box, Tab and ⇧Tab a list's level, ↩ carries a list or a quote on, and lists renumber themselves.
7. **Fonts and size.** A note set in one of eight faces, four serifs and four sans serifs, code in one of four monospace faces, as emaki offers them; the size a preset or your own for the note and for code, Chinese in a carried face of your choice (Noto Serif SC, Noto Sans SC, LXGW WenKai) or one you name from the Mac.
8. **Eight languages.** English, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Japanese, Korean, French, German and Spanish, following the system's.
9. **Updates.** Onyx checks GitHub for a newer release once a day and installs it at a press, with no Sparkle feed.
